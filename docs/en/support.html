Teto Support

Contact us

Open an issue at https://github.com/Gwin14/Teto-condicoes-de-drone/issues describing the problem, your iPhone model and iOS version. We reply within 2 business days.

The score looks wrong

The score uses your selected drone's official limits and your planned altitude (Settings). Check that both are correct. For the current hour, Teto corrects the forecast with the nearest METAR station when one exists.

Location doesn't show up

In iPhone Settings › Privacy › Location Services, check that Teto has permission. You can also pick a city manually.

Widgets and Live Activity

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget and search for Teto. The Live Activity starts when you take off from the Flight tab.

Subscription

To manage or cancel, open iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions. To restore a purchase, use "Restore purchases" in the app.

Disclaimer

Teto is a decision aid. Flight authorization always comes from the country's aviation authority (e.g. FAA, EASA member states, UK CAA).
