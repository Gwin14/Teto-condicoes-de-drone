Teto Privacy Policy

Summary

Teto has no accounts, shows no ads and does not collect personal data on its own servers. Everything you set up (drone, saved spots, flight log, checklist) is stored only on your device.

Location

With your permission, Teto reads your device location to show flying conditions where you are. Rounded coordinates are sent only to the public data services used to build the forecast: Open-Meteo (forecast and place search), aviationweather.gov (METAR and airports), NOAA SWPC (space weather), CelesTrak (satellite orbits), INPE/EMBRACE (scintillation) and ANAC (Brazilian aerodrome registry). These services never receive your name, email or any identifier.

Notifications

Flying-window and battery alerts are local notifications scheduled on your device. We don't use push notifications.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple. To validate your subscription, Teto uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous ID generated by the app and your purchase history, with no personal data. RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Your rights

Because your data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time by removing the app. Privacy questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Gwin14/Condicoes-voo-drone/issues.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date below will be updated. Last updated: November 10, 2026.
